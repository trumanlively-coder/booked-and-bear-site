Customer booking

Booked and Bear is the shop’s owner app. It is where you run the calendar, keep client profiles, and text from the business number. Customers use a separate booking page; they never see this app.

What you can do

  • Calendar that shows the day

    Each day on the calendar shows whether it has appointments or blocked time, so a day is never just a blank square.

  • Block time or add an appointment

    Open a day to block time, or put in an appointment for a person, including their details and the usual appointment actions.

  • Client profiles, A–Z

    Every booking saves a client profile. Search the list, open a person, and see their history.

  • Import from Square

    Bring in clients from a Square Customer Directory CSV. You preview the file and confirm before anything is saved.

  • Today’s roster and texting

    See today’s clients, check the ones you want, and send texts from the business number, (702) 601-8354. Mass texts ask for a confirm first.

  • Buffers and exact service lengths

    Each appointment uses that service’s exact length. You can add a short buffer before or after, which customers never see.
